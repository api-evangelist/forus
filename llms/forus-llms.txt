# Forus

> Forus (formerly Tandem) is an AI-powered healthcare technology company that operates a network connecting physicians, pharmacies, payers, and biopharmaceutical companies to accelerate patient access to prescribed medicines. The platform automates the administrative work between a clinical decision and treatment initiation — insurance prior authorization, denial appeals, financial-assistance enrollment, benefit verification, and pharmacy fulfillment routing — embedded into physician workflows across most U.S. zip codes. Forus does not publish a public developer API, OpenAPI specification, SDKs, or developer documentation; this file is generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from public company pages.
